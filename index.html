🐱 Cat Captain ⛵

Enemies swarm your ship and shoot at the cat. Time your parry — hit Space (or PARRY) just as a shot gets close to swat it back. Wipe out the swarm and kill 3 bosses to win.

1 Player: ←/→ to slide on deck, Space to parry.
2 Player: P1 plays the cat. P2 steers the ship with W A S D to dodge hazards.

⛵ World 1 — High Seas

Parry fly shells on the open ocean. Squash spiders, dodge mines.

🦀 World 2 — Crab Cove

Crabs scuttle in close, then stop and shoot when their eye glows red. Parry the rush and the shots.
